About Me

I'm a self-taught developer working through Python, SQL, and web fundamentals from the ground up -- this site itself is the proof of work. What started as a hand-written HTML page is now a full Flask app with a real SQLite database, automated tests, a CI pipeline, and a Raspberry Pi quietly serving it from my home network. I'd rather learn by building something real and breaking it a few times than follow a tutorial start to finish. Expect posts here about whatever I'm currently stuck on -- SQL, deployment, security, or the next thing I decide to rebuild from scratch.
